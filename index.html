EMBER & ECHO

A two-player platformer. You cannot finish it alone.

Two souls wake in the Hollowlands. Each carries half a broken lantern. Find the Four Shards of the First Dawn — together.

Code:

How to Play

  • Create a room and share the code. Partner joins on another device/tab.
  • Move A/D or arrows · Jump W / Space · Interact E (pick up crates, levers, NPCs).
  • Ember is orange, Echo is teal. Solve puzzles that need both of you.
  • Stand on pressure plates, carry crates, pull levers, step on twin runes together.
  • Collect 4 Shards. Reach the shrine in First Dawn together for the ending.
